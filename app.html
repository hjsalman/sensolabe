How it works

Privacy and method

Nothing leaves this page. Frames from the camera or video file are shrunk to 160 pixels wide and averaged into three numbers per frame: red, green and blue. The picture itself is never stored or sent anywhere.

Pulse. Each heartbeat pushes blood into the skin, which absorbs slightly more green light. The app averages the color of the cheeks and forehead, separates the pulse from lighting changes with a color projection called POS (plane orthogonal to skin), filters to 42 to 180 beats per minute and finds the strongest rhythm.

Breathing (beta). Breathing moves the head and chest slightly, which changes the overall brightness of the box. The app looks for the strongest rhythm between 6 and 30 breaths per minute. Treat it as experimental.

Presence. The speakers play a steady 19 kHz tone and the microphone listens. A still room reflects the tone at the same pitch; anything moving shifts it slightly up or down. The app measures that spread.

Face finding. Skin-colored pixels are located in each frame and the measuring box follows the upper middle of the face. Click the picture to place it yourself.
