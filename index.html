Try it now

Read a heartbeat from an ordinary video

Each heartbeat pushes blood into the skin of your face, which absorbs a little more green light for a split second. A camera can't show it to your eye, but averaged over thousands of pixels it's a clean wave. This runs entirely in your browser; the video never leaves your computer.

Showing a synthetic sample. For your own: film your face for 20 to 30 seconds, sitting still, in daylight, then pick the file. MP4 works best. Click the picture to move the red box onto your cheeks or forehead.

Method: plane-orthogonal-to-skin (POS) color projection, then a 0.7 to 3 Hz band filter and a spectral peak. Published methods reach errors near 1 to 2 BPM on still subjects in good light and get worse with motion, dim light and some skin tones.4 This is a demonstration, not a medical device.

The five senses

What a stock laptop can feel

Each of these has been shown in research or in a shipped product on its own. Sensolabe is one layer that runs all of them together and lets any app ask a simple question: is someone here, are they breathing, what's their pulse.

Camera Proven

Pulse, breathing rate and pulse variability from the face, using the method in the demo above.

Software of this kind received FDA clearance in June 2026.1

Speaker + mic sonar Proven

The speakers play chirps above human hearing; the mic hears the echo. Distance, presence, breathing, a fall, a hand wave. Works in the dark and sees no faces.

Already ships on 27 laptop models for presence only.2

Microphone Research

Snoring and pauses in breathing at night, cough counts over a week. Processed on the device into numbers, so audio is never stored.

Phone-based sleep apnea screening has been shown in university studies.

Keyboard timing Research

How long each key is held, measured in milliseconds during normal typing. Tiny changes have flagged early Parkinson's in studies.

At-home study on people's own laptops: AUC 0.83.5

Wi-Fi Later

Bodies bend Wi-Fi signals. With access to the radio's channel data, motion and breathing can be sensed through walls. Needs chipmaker support, so it comes last.

Fusion The product

Each sense fails somewhere. The camera fails in the dark; sonar can't see a heartbeat; the mic fails in a noisy room. Fused, they cover each other, and each reading comes with a confidence score.

Simulation

Sonar from the speakers you already own

Drag the person around the room. The laptop sends an 18 to 22 kHz chirp, too high for most adults to hear. The echo's delay gives distance: sound travels 343 m/s, and a 4 kHz sweep separates objects about 4 cm apart. Breathing moves the chest 4 to 12 mm, which shows as a wobble in the echo.

Why it works

Three reasons this is possible now

The hardware is already good enough

Laptop cameras record 30 frames a second, which is plenty for a 1 to 3 Hz heartbeat. Laptop speakers and mics handle 48 kHz audio, so 18 to 22 kHz chirps fit under the limit.

Each piece is proven separately

FDA-cleared camera vitals, sonar presence shipping on laptops, keyboard timing in published studies. No one has put them in one layer that every app can call.125

Laptops can run it all offline

New laptop chips include neural engines for local AI. The heavy math runs there, so raw video and audio never leave the device. That's what makes it acceptable in homes, clinics and offices.

What it solves

One update, many problems

The same layer serves very different buyers, because they all want the same three answers: someone's here, they're breathing, here's their pulse.

Telehealth platforms

Vitals in every video visit

The doctor sees pulse and breathing rate during the call, from the patient's own laptop camera.

Senior care, families

A fall alarm from an old tablet

A spare laptop or tablet in the room uses sonar to notice a fall or stopped breathing. One in four US adults over 65 falls each year.6

Sleep clinics

Apnea screening at home

A phone on the nightstand listens and pings through the night, then flags who needs a real sleep study.3

Laptop makers

Presence without a sensor

Lock when you walk away, wake when you return, dim the screen to save battery. Saves the cost of a dedicated sensor on every unit.2

Neurology research

Early signs in everyday typing

Opt-in keyboard timing tracked over months, sent to a study team as summary numbers, never as keystrokes.5
